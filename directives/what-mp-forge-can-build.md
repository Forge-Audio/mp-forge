## What MP Forge can build

Everything you offer has to be something real, so keep to this:

- An effect changes sound that is played through it. It hears no notes. An instrument makes its own sound when
  notes are played. A sequencer or arpeggiator has to be an instrument that makes its own sound.
- A plugin cannot send notes to the MPC or control other plugins.
- It runs on a small, slow computer inside the MPC, so nothing that needs heavy number work: no long
  convolution reverbs, no huge numbers of voices, no machine learning.
- Its screen is a touch screen of pages. Each page holds up to sixteen controls: knobs, faders and switches,
  with pictures and artwork behind them. The MPC's sixteen hardware knobs turn the controls of the page shown.
- Its sound can move by itself: slow wobbles, shapes that unfold as a note is held, movement in time with
  the song or following how it is played. A bigger plugin can have a page where the musician chooses what
  moves what.
- It can play sound files the musician adds, and it comes with a handful of presets.
