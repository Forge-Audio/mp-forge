A plugin for the MPCs with a touch screen, such as the Live, One, X and Key: an effect or an instrument.
An app is your own OS for the MPC Sample. It takes over the whole device: the screen, 16 pads, 3 knobs, fader, dial and buttons, and all the sound.
It makes its sound in code, and can play sound files you add with + or pick from the SD card. It runs here first, on a drawing of the device or on your own MPC Sample linked by its cable (see Settings). Once it passes its safety check, the send button puts it on your MPC Sample to run there by itself.

SETTINGS

GENERAL

Colour mode
Font
MP Forge is written in pixel letters. If they are hard to read, choose PLAIN: MP Forge then uses your computer's own font. A plugin's screen is not changed by this.
Animations
The conversation sliding aside, the blacksmith at work and the like. If motion on screen bothers you, choose AS MY COMPUTER SAYS: MP Forge then keeps still whenever your computer is set to show less motion, as on Windows with Animation effects off, or on a Mac with Reduce motion on.

PLUGINS

Where your plugins are kept
Each plugin is a folder of its own on this computer, holding its code, its screen, its versions and your conversation about it. You can keep them in any folder you like, for instance one that gets backed up.
Versions kept of each plugin
A version is kept before every change, so you can always go back. With a limit, a plugin's oldest versions are thrown away for good once it has more than that. You can also delete single versions from VERSIONS.
Online plugins EXPERIMENTAL
Lets a plugin bring in sound from the internet, such as a radio station. A plugin's own code still never goes online: it lists the addresses it wants, and MP Forge's own helper fetches sound from those and from nowhere else. Such a plugin is marked ONLINE, with its addresses. While this is off, a plugin like that is not built or sent, and it stays silent when you play it here. Sound from the internet is decoded inside your MPC's app, which can never be as safe as staying offline, so leave this off unless you want such a plugin. On Windows a stream is silent in MP Forge and plays on the MPC.

SEND SETTINGS

Your MPC
Used when you send a plugin to it. The MPC shows its address under its Wi-Fi settings, and has to have developer mode on. A plugin only ever goes on a USB drive or SD card in the MPC, never on its built-in storage.
Password, if it asks for one
Your MPC Sample EXPERIMENTAL
Makes a real MPC Sample, on its USB-C cable, the screen and controls of an app running here, and takes an app you send onto the card in it. It needs the boot menu SampleBoot on its SD card, and has been tried on firmware 1.3.0.12 only.
Put the card in this computer, press the button and choose the card.

AI

AI accounts
A key is kept in this computer's own store for secrets: the keychain on a Mac, the Credential Manager on Windows, the keyring on Linux. It is never written into a plugin's files and the AI is never shown it. Claude works without a key, through Claude Code's own sign-in.
AI context
The context is everything the AI is holding in mind while it works on one request: your words, the files it has read, the pictures it was shown. It is counted in tokens and shown at the top of the conversation. Past the limit you set here, what the AI read longest ago is dropped to make room (compaction). It can read anything again when it needs to. A lower limit costs less and keeps the AI focused. A higher one lets it hold more at once.
