Where your plugins are kept
Each plugin is a folder of its own on this computer, holding its code, its screen, its versions and your conversation about it. You can keep them in any folder you like, for instance one that gets backed up.
Versions kept of each plugin
A version is kept before every change, so you can always go back. With a limit, a plugin's oldest versions are thrown away for good once it has more than that. You can also delete single versions from VERSIONS.
AI context
The context is everything the AI is holding in mind while it works on one request: your words, the files it has read, the pictures it was shown. It is counted in tokens and shown at the top of the conversation. Past the limit you set here, what the AI read longest ago is dropped to make room (compaction). It can read anything again when it needs to. A lower limit costs less and keeps the AI focused. A higher one lets it hold more at once.
What MP Forge needs on this computer
MP Forge builds and checks every plugin on this computer, with Akai's own tools. This is what those tools need, and whether it is here.
Your MPC
Used when you send a plugin to it. The MPC shows its address under its Wi-Fi settings, and has to have developer mode on. A plugin only ever goes on a USB drive or SD card in the MPC, never on its built-in storage.
Password, if it asks for one
AI accounts
A key is kept in this computer's own store for secrets: the keychain on a Mac, the Credential Manager on Windows, the keyring on Linux. It is never written into a plugin's files and the AI is never shown it. Claude works without a key, through Claude Code's own sign-in.
